# Inventory and supplier analytics in Excel and Power Query

## The problem
Faisal Textiles (Manufacturing) came to me because buyers did not know which suppliers were late or which stock was dead.

## What I built
Over 3 weeks I delivered an automated Excel model with supplier on-time scores and ABC/XYZ inventory classification. The work used better spreadsheets, Power Query, Power Pivot.

## Results
- dead stock identified worth about PKR 18M
- supplier on-time delivery up from 71% to 86%

> "Simple tool that the buyers actually use." - Faisal Textiles team
