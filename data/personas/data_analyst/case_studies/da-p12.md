# Insight Pakistan: survey analysis in 2024

When Insight Pakistan reached out, a 5,000-respondent survey needed segments the client could act on. I spent 3 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was weighted responses, factor analysis on attitude questions and k-means segments with personas, built with Python, cleaning data in Python, scikit-learn, Excel.

The part I am proudest of: five segments adopted in the client's brand strategy. Report delivered three days ahead of deadline.
