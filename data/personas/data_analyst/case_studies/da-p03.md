# Snackbox: churn analysis in 2024

When Snackbox reached out, the team knew churn was rising but not which customers or why. I spent 4 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was cohort retention curves, survival analysis by acquisition channel and a Tableau story for leadership, built with Python, cleaning data in Python, SQL, Tableau.

The part I am proudest of: identified a discount channel with 2x churn, which was paused. 12-month retention up 6 points the next quarter.
