# Sales performance dashboard for a consumer-goods distributor

## The problem
Shan Distributors (FMCG) came to me because regional managers waited a week for an Excel pack that was already out of date.

## What I built
Over 5 weeks I delivered a star-schema model refreshed daily with a Power BI report per region and row-level security. The work used an interactive Microsoft dashboard for managers, SQL Server, DAX, Excel.

## Results
- reporting lag from 7 days to 1 day
- used weekly by 60 managers

> "Our managers finally look at the same numbers." - Shan Distributors team
