# Case study: Bykea Partners

**Situation.** Supply and demand were matched by gut feeling across 40 zones.
**Task.** Deliver a production-ready fix within 5 weeks for a transport business.
**Action.** A geospatial dashboard of supply, demand and wait times by zone and hour with alerting on gaps. Tooling: interactive visual reports for leadership, PostgreSQL, SQL, Python.
**Result.** average rider wait time down 18%; dashboard adopted by 25 zone managers.
