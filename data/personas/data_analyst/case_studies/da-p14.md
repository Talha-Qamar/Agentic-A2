# Case study: Teamboard

**Situation.** The product team could not say where trial users dropped off.
**Task.** Deliver a production-ready fix within 4 weeks for a saas business.
**Action.** Designed an event taxonomy, fixed tracking gaps and built activation and retention funnels. Tooling: Mixpanel, writing queries against our database, Python, Segment.
**Result.** found an onboarding step losing 38% of trials; trial-to-paid conversion up from 9% to 13%.
