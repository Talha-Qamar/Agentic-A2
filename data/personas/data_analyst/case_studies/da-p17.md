# Case study: Dawn Digital

**Situation.** An analyst spent every Monday copying numbers into an email.
**Task.** Deliver a production-ready fix within 2 weeks for a media business.
**Action.** A scheduled script that queries the database, renders charts and sends a templated KPI email. Tooling: Python, writing queries against our database, Google Sheets API.
**Result.** saved about 5 hours of analyst time a week; report sent at 8 am every Monday without fail.
