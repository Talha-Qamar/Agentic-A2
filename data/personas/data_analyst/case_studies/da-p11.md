# Case study: RouteMate

**Situation.** The company ran on 40 linked Google Sheets that broke weekly.
**Task.** Deliver a production-ready fix within 7 weeks for a logistics business.
**Action.** Ingested operational sources into BigQuery, modelled them in dbt and rebuilt the key reports in Looker Studio. Tooling: Google's data warehouse, dbt, Python, Looker Studio.
**Result.** retired 40 spreadsheets; report refresh from manual weekly to automatic hourly.
