# FreshCart: pricing analytics in 2025

When FreshCart reached out, discounts were set by intuition and margins were shrinking. I spent 5 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was estimated price elasticity by category with controls for promotions and seasonality and simulated margin impact, built with Python, statsmodels, writing queries against our database, Tableau.

The part I am proudest of: recommended price changes lifted gross margin 1.8 points in the test categories. Discount depth reduced on inelastic categories.
