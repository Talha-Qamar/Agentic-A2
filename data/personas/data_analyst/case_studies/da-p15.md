# Akhuwat Finance: risk analytics in 2024

When Akhuwat Finance reached out, portfolio-at-risk was calculated monthly in Excel and often wrong. I spent 6 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was automated PAR, roll-rate and vintage analysis with a Power BI dashboard for the risk committee, built with writing queries against our database, Power BI, Python.

The part I am proudest of: PAR reporting from monthly to daily. Regulatory report errors reduced to zero in two audits.
