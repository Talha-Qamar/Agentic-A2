# Demand forecast for a pharmacy chain

## The problem
CarePlus Pharmacy (Healthcare) came to me because pharmacies ran out of seasonal medicines during flu season.

## What I built
Over 6 weeks I delivered weekly SKU forecasts with Ramadan and flu-season effects, exported to the purchasing team's Excel template. The work used Python, Prophet, writing queries against our database, Excel.

## Results
- stock-outs on top 200 SKUs down 35%
- forecast error (WAPE) from 28% to 16%

> "Purchasing trusts the forecast now." - CarePlus Pharmacy team
