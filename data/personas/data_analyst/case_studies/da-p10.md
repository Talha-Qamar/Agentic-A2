# HR attrition analytics for a BPO

## The problem
TeleConnect BPO (HR) came to me because agent attrition was 60% a year and exit reasons were never analysed.

## What I built
Over 5 weeks I delivered combined HRIS, attendance and survey data into an attrition dashboard with drivers by team lead. The work used an interactive Microsoft dashboard for managers, Excel, SQL.

## Results
- identified two team leads driving a third of exits
- annualised attrition down to 47% in nine months

> "Gave us facts for hard conversations." - TeleConnect BPO team
