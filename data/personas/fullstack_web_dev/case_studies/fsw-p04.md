# Headless Shopify storefront rebuild

## The problem
Zari Studio (E-commerce) came to me because a theme-based store scored 31 on mobile Lighthouse and lost shoppers on slow pages.

## What I built
Over 7 weeks I delivered a headless Next.js storefront with incremental static regeneration, image optimisation and a Shopify checkout. The work used TypeScript, a fast, SEO-friendly React website, Shopify Storefront API, Tailwind CSS, Vercel.

## Results
- mobile Lighthouse score from 31 to 94
- conversion rate up 19% month over month

> "The site is fast now and sales followed." - Zari Studio team
