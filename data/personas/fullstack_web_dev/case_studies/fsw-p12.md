# Pipelyne: browser extension in 2025

When Pipelyne reached out, reps kept prospect notes in spreadsheets that never synced. I spent 4 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was a Manifest V3 extension with a side panel, offline-first storage and sync to a Supabase backend, built with TypeScript, a browser add-on for our sales team, React, Supabase.

The part I am proudest of: 1,200 weekly active users after launch. Chrome Web Store rating of 4.7.
