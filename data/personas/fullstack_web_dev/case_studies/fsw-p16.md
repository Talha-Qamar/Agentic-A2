# Analytics dashboard with charts and CSV exports

## The problem
KhetiTech (Agriculture) came to me because agronomists emailed spreadsheets around to compare field yields.

## What I built
Over 5 weeks I delivered a dashboard with yield maps, season comparisons and scheduled CSV exports backed by a FastAPI service. The work used a modern single-page frontend, TypeScript, D3.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL.

## Results
- weekly reporting time from 6 hours to 30 minutes
- used by 85 agronomists

> "Exactly the charts our agronomists asked for." - KhetiTech team
