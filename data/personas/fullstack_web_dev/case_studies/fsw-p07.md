# Marketing site and CMS migration to a headless CMS

## The problem
Roshan Foundation (Non-profit) came to me because volunteers could not update a WordPress site without breaking the layout.

## What I built
Over 4 weeks I delivered a Sanity-backed Next.js site with structured content, English/Urdu localisation and WCAG AA checks. The work used a fast, SEO-friendly React website, Sanity, Tailwind CSS, Vercel.

## Results
- content edits by non-developers went from 0 to about 30 a month
- page weight down 70%

> "Our volunteers can update the site themselves." - Roshan Foundation team
