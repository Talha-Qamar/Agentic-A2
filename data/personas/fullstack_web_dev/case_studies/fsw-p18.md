# Docstack: AI integration in 2025

When Docstack reached out, users could not find answers in 300 help-centre articles. I spent 4 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was an embedded chat widget with streaming answers grounded in help articles and a handoff to human support, built with TypeScript, a fast, SEO-friendly React website, the OpenAI API, PostgreSQL, pgvector.

The part I am proudest of: support tickets down 29%. Answer helpfulness rated 4.4/5 by users.
