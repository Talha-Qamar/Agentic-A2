# Booking marketplace MVP for home services

## The problem
FixIt.pk (Home Services) came to me because the founders needed a working two-sided marketplace to raise a seed round.

## What I built
Over 12 weeks I delivered an MVP with provider onboarding, location-based search, split payments and reviews in 12 weeks. The work used Python, a Python backend with an admin panel, React, PostgreSQL, Stripe Connect.

## Results
- launched with 180 providers in two cities
- the company raised its seed round on the MVP

> "Delivered the MVP on time and it held up during the launch." - FixIt.pk team
