# Multi-tenant SaaS dashboard for a logistics startup

## The problem
RouteMate (Logistics) came to me because each customer got a hand-patched copy of the app and onboarding took a week.

## What I built
Over 10 weeks I delivered a single multi-tenant Next.js app with tenant-scoped queries, role-based permissions and self-serve onboarding. The work used TypeScript, a fast, SEO-friendly React website, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma.

## Results
- new customer onboarding from 5 days to 20 minutes
- infrastructure cost per tenant down 60%

> "One codebase instead of twelve forks, finally." - RouteMate team
