# Case study: PeopleFirst

**Situation.** HR answered the same 50 leave and benefits questions every week.
**Task.** Deliver a production-ready fix within 5 weeks for a hr business.
**Action.** A Teams bot that answers from the policy wiki and filters documents by the employee's country and grade. Tooling: Python, LlamaIndex, Azure OpenAI, Microsoft Teams.
**Result.** HR ticket volume down 41% in three months; no policy answers leaked across regions in the audit.
