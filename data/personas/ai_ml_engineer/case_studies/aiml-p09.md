# NorthBay Capital: multi-agent in 2025

When NorthBay Capital reached out, first drafts of company notes took analysts most of a day. I spent 8 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was planner, researcher and critic agents with a bounded revision loop and source links on every paragraph, built with Python, a stateful agent workflow with branching steps and a human review step, Tavily API, Pinecone, Streamlit.

The part I am proudest of: draft time cut from about 6 hours to 35 minutes. Analysts kept 70% of generated paragraphs.
