# ZongPlus: churn prediction in 2024

When ZongPlus reached out, the retention team called customers at random and burned their offer budget. I spent 7 weeks on it. The heart of the solution was a churn model with calibrated probabilities and a simple uplift ranking so offers went to persuadable customers, built with Python, scikit-learn, writing queries against our database, Power BI.

The part I am proudest of: retention campaign ROI up 2.3x. AUC of 0.84 on the next quarter's data.
